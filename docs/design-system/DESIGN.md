## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an energetic, conversion-optimized retail experience balancing high consumer trust with promotional excitement. It blends modern corporate reliability with tactile retail dynamism—combining punchy, warm golden-amber accents with structural clarity and crisp white container surfaces.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Everyday online shoppers seeking dependable value, lightning-fast navigation, clear pricing transparency, and modern curated storefront aesthetics.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires urgency without anxiety; establishes immediate credibility through clean typographic hierarchy, upfront discount metrics, and prominent trust guarantees.

### Design Movements & Visual Language
- **Modern Retail Pragmatism:** High-contrast data density, prominent micro-badges (-44%, Featured), and distinct interactive layers.
- **Crisp Surface Framing:** Clean container architecture elevated slightly against subtle off-white backgrounds (`#f8fafc`), framed by soft borders (`#e2e8f0`) to avoid visual fatigue across dense product grids.
- **Warm Kinetic Accents:** Intense amber golds paired with electric brand utility colors (Prime purple, Trade-in emerald, and Alert crimson) define priority actions instantly.

## Colors

The palette balances intense call-to-action vitality with rigorous utility and readability.

### Key Palettes & Roles
- **Primary / Brand Action (`#f59e0b` / `#ea580c`):** Drives primary commerce actions (Add to Cart, Buy Now, Search Confirm, Hero Highlights). Paired with `#0f172a` text or stark white for optimal accessibility.
- **Deal & Urgency Accent (`#ef4444` / `#dc2626`):** Reserved strictly for savings, markdown badges (`-38%`), limited-time tags, and stock urgency.
- **Service & Membership Accents:**
  - *Z Prime Violet (`#7c3aed` to `#6366f1`):* Used for loyalty tiers, expedited delivery perks, and exclusive membership blocks.
  - *Trade & Save Emerald (`#059669` to `#10b981`):* Denotes circular economy promotions, trade-ins, and sustainability programs.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Dark Charcoal Navy (`#0f172a` to `#111827`): Anchor zones such as the primary masthead, utility bar, and rich footer.
  - Canvas & Neutral Tiers: Page background is `#f8fafc`; cards and active sheets are pure `#ffffff`. Structural divides use `#e2e8f0` and `#e5e7eb`.
  - Content Text: Primary content uses `#0f172a`, while secondary specs, review tallies, and strikethrough original prices use `#64748b`.

## Typography

The type system blends the energetic geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headers, prices, and navigation labels with the structural clarity of **Inter** for descriptions, reviews, and specs.

### Hierarchy & Numerals
- **Pricing:** Price displays utilize bold weights with tightened letter-spacing (`price-hero`) to anchor product evaluation. Strikethrough comparison prices (`price-strike`) sit alongside in muted slate tones.
- **Micro-copy & Badges:** Badges (`label-badge`) are uppercase or bold-condensed to remain legible at minute scales (10px–11px) inside high-contrast pill markers.
- **Product Titles:** Limited to 2-line clamps using `headline-sm` or `body-md` bold to maintain alignment uniformity across card shelves.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive grid governs the catalog experience with a maximum layout bound of 1360px.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adapters
- **Desktop (≥1200px):** 12-column grid; `gutter-lg` (24px); 4-column product shelves; 10-column category strip; 4-up horizontal trust band.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout; `gutter` (16px); 3-column product grid; 6-column category bar with horizontal scroll overflow.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout; `margin` (16px); 2-column compact product card grid; scrollable swipe categories; stacked trust banners.

### Spacing Density Principles
- Card internal padding is tightly standardized to `space-md` (16px) around the metadata section, while product images extend full-bleed edge-to-edge within their top frame.
- Sections (e.g., Today's Deals, Featured Collections) maintain a systematic `space-xl` (36px) vertical gap to separate high-velocity merchandising layers clearly.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages a hybrid model of crisp low-contrast borders and feather-weight ambient shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#f8fafc` flat background for the entire application viewport.
- **Level 1 (Default Containers & Tiles):** Pure white `#ffffff` cards framed with a 1px border (`#e2e8f0`). No drop shadow in resting state to preserve visual cleanliness in multi-item lists.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover States & Trust Bars):** Resting state applies `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Hovering over product cards transitions to `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` and elevates by -2px vertically.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Masthead & Drawers):** Global search header and department bar use `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` with backdrop clarity.
- **Level 4 (Floating Modals & AI Assistants):** Components such as the "Ask Zoe" assistive pill and quick-cart popovers utilize deep ambient dispersion: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The interface adopts a approachable, friendly curvature standard (`roundedness: 2`, where base elements are 8px / 0.5rem) providing modern consumer appeal while sustaining tight spatial efficiency.

### Shape Applications
- **Base Cards & Hero Units:** 12px–16px (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`) for container surfaces, promo banners, and hero carousel frames.
- **Interactive Controls:** Form inputs, main category icon squares, and Add-to-Cart buttons feature an 8px (`rounded-md`) radius.
- **Tags, Discount Pills & Micro-CTAs:** 9999px (full pill curvature) on badges (e.g., `-44%`, `Featured`), quick-action floating buttons, and floating support triggers (`Ask Zoe`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (CTA / Add to Cart):** Background `#f59e0b` transitioning to `#d97706` on hover. Bold `#0f172a` text, full container width within cards, 40px height, 8px border radius. Focused with an offset 2px ring in amber.
- **Secondary (Outline / Ghost):** Transparent fill, 1px border `#e2e8f0`, text `#0f172a`, subtle grey hover `#f1f5f9`.
- **Special Membership CTA:** Full-gradient fills (`#7c3aed` to `#6366f1` for Z Prime; `#059669` to `#10b981` for Trade & Save) with pure white text and soft glow hover transitions.

### Product Card
- **Frame:** Solid white background, 1px border `#e2e8f0`, 12px radius, overflow hidden.
- **Visuals & Badges:** 1:1 aspect ratio image container with neutral backdrop (`#f8fafc`). Top-left contains stacked absolute-positioned pills (`Featured` in amber, discount `-44%` in crimson `#ef4444`). Top-right holds the circular wishlist heart toggle button (white circle, subtle shadow, light grey outline).
- **Metadata Stack:** Brand name in uppercase `11px` muted slate, followed by a 2-line clamped product name in `14px` dark text. 5-star rating icon strip in `#f59e0b` paired with count in parentheses `(28,743)`.
- **Price Block:** Bold primary price (`$14.99`) alongside crossed-out MSRP (`$27`) in muted slate.
- **Card Footer:** Persistent full-width amber "Add to cart" button.

### Category Icon Pills
- Centered vertical layout with a 64x64px soft square container (`#f8fafc` or faint pastel tints), 12px radius, housing 32px iconography or 3D product renders. Below sits a 12px semi-bold label with 1-line truncation.

### Search Masthead & Department Nav
- **Main Bar:** Rich dark slate or golden brand header housing the logo mark, delivery location pill, full-width segmented search input (category dropdown + text field + search magnifying glass button), and profile/cart counters.
- **Secondary Nav:** High-density horizontal bar displaying "Today's Deals" in bold red/accent pill accompanied by department links (`Audio`, `Fashion`, `Electronics`) with subtle hover underlines.

### Trust & Guarantee 4-Up Grid
- Horizontal white cards containing centered or start-aligned icons inside soft amber circles, featuring a 2-line structure: Bold guarantee title (`Free Shipping`) and explanatory sub-detail (`On orders over $99`).
